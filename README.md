# Varnix

**Billing, stock and reports for Indian shops — works offline, on Windows and Android.**

Point of sale with barcode scanning, GST invoices and GSTR-1 export, stock and purchases, customers with credit (udhaar) and loyalty points, smart reorder alerts, daily reports, encrypted backups, staff PINs — in English, हिन्दी, मराठी, ગુજરાતી, தமிழ் and తెలుగు.
