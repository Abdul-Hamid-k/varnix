# Varnix test version: guide for testers

Thank you for testing Varnix! This is a **test version**. It is the full shop app: billing, stock, customers, reports and backups. It works without internet.

## 1. Download

Open the download page and choose your device (x.y.z is the version number, for example 0.2.1):

**https://github.com/Abdul-Hamid-k/varnix/releases/latest**

| Device | File |
|---|---|
| Windows computer (Windows 10 or 11) | `Varnix-Setup-x.y.z.exe` |
| Android phone or tablet (Android 7 or newer) | `Varnix-x.y.z.apk` |

## 2. Install

### Windows

1. Open `Varnix-Setup-x.y.z.exe`.
2. Windows may show **"Windows protected your PC"**. This is normal for a test version. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
3. Follow the steps (Next → Install → Finish). Varnix opens.

### Android

1. Open `Varnix-x.y.z.apk` from your downloads.
2. If the phone asks, allow **Install unknown apps** for your browser or file app, then tap **Install**.
3. If Play Protect warns you, tap **More details → Install anyway**.

## 3. Start

1. **Register your shop**: owner's name, shop name, mobile number and city (email is optional). You need internet only for this step. Your **15-day free trial** starts.
2. **First-time setup**: store details and the owner's PIN. Choose **Start fresh** for your real shop, or **Explore with demo data** to look around first (you can remove the demo data later from the dashboard).
3. Start billing.

## 4. What to try

Please use Varnix the way you run your shop, and try as many of these as you can:

- [ ] Add 10 or more of your products (with GST rate and stock).
- [ ] Make bills at the counter: search, barcode scan (camera on phones), discounts, cash/UPI/card, credit (udhaar).
- [ ] Print a bill or share it as PDF on WhatsApp.
- [ ] Return an item, and void a wrong bill (owner).
- [ ] Add stock, record damaged or expired stock.
- [ ] Add a supplier and a purchase order; receive the stock.
- [ ] Add customers; check their visits and loyalty points.
- [ ] Look at the dashboard, Smart alerts and Reports (daily report, GST return).
- [ ] Add a staff user with their own PIN, and check what staff can and cannot do.
- [ ] Take a backup (Backup → Back up now). On a phone, share it to yourself.
- [ ] Change the language (हिन्दी, मराठी, ગુજરાતી, தமிழ், తెలుగు) and try dark mode.

A full manual with pictures is included: **Varnix-User-Manual.pdf** on the download page.
